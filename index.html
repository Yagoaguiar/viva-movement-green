BEFORE YOU GET STARTED

A few questions?
Let’s talk.

Do I need any training experience?

No. Your plan should reflect your current fitness level, including if you are a complete beginner. Your trainer will discuss your needs and any limitations during your initial conversation.

Can I train at home?

Training can be adapted for home or the gym. Before you start, let your trainer know what space and equipment you have so you can agree on a suitable setup.

How does online coaching work?

Both plans include app access, a personalised workout plan and a monthly training plan review. Essential includes one 30-minute Microsoft Teams call per month. Premium includes two calls per month, weekly progress reviews and message support. Your coach will confirm messaging hours and response times before you sign up.

Does coaching include a meal plan?

This coaching focuses on exercise. Premium includes a library of vegan recipes for inspiration, not an individualised diet. For a personalised meal plan, speak with a qualified dietitian.

How soon will I see results?

Everyone’s journey is different. Results depend on many factors, so there is no guaranteed timeframe or amount of weight loss. The focus is on building habits you can maintain.
